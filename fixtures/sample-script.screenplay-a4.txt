06|               INT. LIGHTHOUSE - LAMP ROOM - NIGHT
08|               The great lamp turns. Every few seconds its beam sweeps
09|               across MAGGIE (60s), wrapped in a coat two sizes too big,
10|               writing in a logbook by torchlight.
12|               She checks the barometer, taps it, and writes again.
14|                                     MAGGIE (V.O.)
15|                         Fourteenth of November. Wind from
16|                         the south-west, forty knots and
17|                         rising. Glass falling. No ships.
19|               She closes the book. Below, the sea throws itself against
20|               the rocks.
22|               SILENCE.
24|               Then a RADIO crackles on the shelf.
26|                                     RADIO (O.S.)
27|                         Low Tide Light, Low Tide Light,
28|                         this is coastguard. Do you read?
30|               Maggie lifts the handset.
32|                                     MAGGIE
33|                               (into handset)
34|                         Coastguard, this is Low Tide.
35|                         Reading you fine.
37|               She waits. Nothing.
39|                                     MAGGIE (CONT'D)
40|                         Coastguard?
42|               THE STAIRWELL
44|               Ninety-nine iron steps spiral down into the dark. Maggie
45|               takes them two at a time, which at her age is a mistake.
47|               INT. LIGHTHOUSE - KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS
49|               A kettle on a gas ring. A table for one, laid for two out
50|               of habit. TOM (40s), soaked to the skin, sits in the
51|               second chair as if he never left.

54|                         You still lay my place. Dad wrote
